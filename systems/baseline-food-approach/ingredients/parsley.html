Parsley (Dried)

TypeDried culinary herb (Petroselinum crispum)

Dried parsley is a mild green herb used for background freshness and colour. Drying greatly changes the bright character of fresh parsley, producing a subtler and more shelf-stable seasoning.

Nutrition & composition

  • Parsley is naturally rich in several micronutrients by weight, including vitamin K, but dried culinary portions are small.
  • Contains plant pigments and aromatic compounds.
  • Its normal culinary role is seasoning rather than serving as a major nutrient source.

Culinary characteristics

  • Milder and less grassy than fresh parsley.
  • Rehydrates readily in soups, sauces and stews.
  • Works well as a supporting herb alongside stronger seasonings.

Uses & preparation

  • Soups, sauces, stews and casseroles.
  • Potato, grain, pulse and vegetable dishes.
  • Herb blends and savoury coatings.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Store airtight away from light and moisture.
  • Add earlier for integration or later for a more noticeable herb note.
  • People taking warfarin should keep vitamin-K-rich foods reasonably consistent overall rather than making abrupt large dietary changes.
