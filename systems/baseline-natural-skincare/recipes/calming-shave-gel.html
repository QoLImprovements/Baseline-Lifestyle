Calming Shave Gel

Recipe Ingredient Information

Ingredients

  • 240 ml Water
  • 2 tablespoons Slippery Elm Powder
  • 5 ml Vegetable Glycerin
  • Guar Gum – enough to thicken to the preferred gel consistency
  • 1.25 ml Jojoba Oil

Method

  1. Combine: Add the slippery elm powder to the water and mix thoroughly.
  2. Hydrate: Allow it to hydrate and develop its natural mucilage.
  3. Add glycerin: Mix in the vegetable glycerin.
  4. Thicken: Gradually add guar gum until the desired shaving-gel thickness is reached.
  5. Add jojoba: Mix in the jojoba oil thoroughly.

Storage & Use

  • Store appropriately and use a suitable preservative if keeping the water-based gel for more than immediate use.

Comedogenic Considerations

  • Mostly water-based with a small jojoba phase. Keep use rinse-off and monitor individual skin response if congestion-prone.

Notes

  • Guar gum is the texture adjustment: add it gradually rather than treating thickness as a fixed target.

Purpose

A shaving gel designed to provide wet glide, mucilage cushion and a small emollient phase so the blade moves with less drag.

Formula Profile

Water-based • rinse-off • slippery-elm/guar gel • small jojoba phase • unpreserved unless a preservation system is added.

How the Formulation Works

Slippery elm supplies mucilage and cushioning; glycerin adds glide and water retention; guar builds gel structure; jojoba adds light lubrication; water hydrates the gums.

Design Rationale

Slippery elm provides the main shave slip, with guar added only until the preferred structure is reached.

Variables & Fixed Elements

Guar gum is added gradually to the preferred consistency. Water, slippery elm, glycerin and jojoba form the fixed base.

Boundaries

A water-based gel needs suitable preservation if stored rather than made for immediate use. It is intended as a rinse-off shaving medium.
