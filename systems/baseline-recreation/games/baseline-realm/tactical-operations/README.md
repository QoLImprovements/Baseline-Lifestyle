# Tactical Operations

Tactical Operations is a complete seven-mission grid tactics campaign inside Baseline Realm.

## Campaign

1. **Watchtower Break** — capture the signal tile.
2. **Cinderline Sweep** — eliminate the ash-road raiders.
3. **Hearthwall Hold** — defend the beacon through round five.
4. **Quarry Extraction** — reach the far extraction point before the route closes.
5. **Lantern Rescue** — reach the trapped scout within the rescue window.
6. **Fogbound Vigil** — survive six rounds of advancing pressure.
7. **Hollow-Crown Siege** — defeat the Hollow-Crown Warden.

Missions unlock sequentially and preserve campaign completion, party experience, job levels, Workshop consumables and one-time rewards in the shared Realm save. The module includes terrain height, movement and jump limits, line of sight, facing and positional bonuses, area abilities, status zones, a visible turn timeline, keyboard and pointer input, and a six-character roster with four-character deployment.

## Completion contract

Every advertised mission family is implemented and covered by the Tactical Operations campaign test. Runtime presentation uses module-owned interface styling and does not depend on the removed shared legacy-art directory.
