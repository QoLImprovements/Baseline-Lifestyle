Brazil Nut Butter

TypeTree-nut butter made from Brazil nuts (Bertholletia excelsa)

Brazil nut butter is a rich, oily nut paste with a distinctive creamy flavour. Like whole Brazil nuts it is unusually concentrated in selenium, while also supplying fat, protein and other minerals.

Nutrition & composition

  • Provides mostly fat, much of it unsaturated, together with plant protein and some fibre.
  • Brazil nuts are exceptionally selenium-rich; selenium content can vary substantially with growing conditions.
  • Also contributes minerals such as magnesium and copper.

Culinary characteristics

  • Very rich and fluid compared with some nut butters because of its high oil content.
  • Distinctive nut flavour can dominate delicate preparations.
  • Can enrich spreads, sauces, porridge and blended foods.

Uses & preparation

  • Spreads and breakfast bowls.
  • Nut-based sauces, dressings and desserts.
  • Blended with other nuts where a milder overall flavour is wanted.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Because selenium is highly concentrated, large regular amounts are not appropriate simply to obtain more nutrition.
  • Natural oil separation is normal; stir before use and store away from heat and light.
  • Tree-nut allergen.
