Lip/ balm

Yield: 100 g. Anhydrous vegan lip/body balm. The default formula is fixed at the balanced reference point; the sliders let the viewer change two physical qualities while preserving a 100 g batch.

Butter character
15% mango • 10% kokum
CreamierFirmer
Structure
3% candelilla • 25.5% apricot kernel oil
Softer meltMore durable set
Overall Feel

Balanced rich balm with a smooth set, controlled glide and durable protective finish.

Ingredients

  • Butters
  • 20 g Cocoa Butter
  • 15 g Mango Butter
  • 10 g Kokum Butter
  • Oils
  • 25.5 g Apricot Kernel Oil
  • 15 g Avocado Oil
  • 11 g Red Raspberry Seed Oil
  • Structure & antioxidant
  • 3 g Candelilla Wax
  • 0.5 g Vitamin E (Tocopherol)

Method

  1. Prepare: Clean and completely dry the working area, utensils, heat-safe container and final balm containers.
  2. Weigh: Set the sliders to the desired positions, then weigh the cocoa butter, mango butter, kokum butter, candelilla wax and liquid oils in the amounts shown.
  3. Melt: Gently heat the cocoa butter, mango butter, kokum butter and candelilla wax using a water bath or double boiler until just fully melted.
  4. Combine: Remove from direct heat, add the apricot kernel, avocado and red raspberry seed oils, and stir until completely uniform.
  5. Add vitamin E: Allow the mixture to cool slightly while remaining fully liquid, then stir in the vitamin E thoroughly.
  6. Pour: Pour into clean, dry containers and leave undisturbed until completely set and cool.
  7. Cap: Fit the lids once the balm is fully cool and label the batch.

Storage & Use

  • Apply a thin layer to lips or dry areas as needed.
  • Store cool, dry and away from direct sunlight or sustained heat.
  • Because the formula is anhydrous, keep water out of the container during use.

Comedogenic Considerations

  • This is a rich, persistent balm designed primarily for lips and targeted dry areas rather than as a lightweight full-face moisturiser. Individual skin response varies.

Notes

  • Default formula: 20% cocoa butter • 15% mango butter • 10% kokum butter • 25.5% apricot kernel oil • 15% avocado oil • 11% red raspberry seed oil • 3% candelilla wax • 0.5% vitamin E.
  • Butter character slider: changes mango against kokum while keeping their combined share fixed at 25%.
  • Structure slider: changes candelilla against apricot kernel oil while keeping their combined share fixed at 28.5%.
  • Vitamin E is an antioxidant for the oil phase, not a broad-spectrum preservative.
  • Use cosmetic-grade raw materials suitable for lip-care applications when the product is intended for lips.
