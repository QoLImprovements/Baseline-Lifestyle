Natural Tooth-Brightening Powder Recipe

Testing — formulation currently being tested before promotion into the main recipe collection.

Ingredients

  • 24 g Superfine Food/Pharmaceutical-Grade White Kaolin Clay
  • 4.5 g Very Finely Powdered Sage Leaf
  • 1.5 g Very Finely Powdered Peppermint Leaf

Ingredient Roles

  • White Kaolin Clay – provides gentle polishing to help remove surface staining and brighten the appearance of the teeth.
  • Sage Leaf Powder – traditional oral-care herb that complements the cleansing powder.
  • Peppermint Leaf Powder – provides natural freshness and a mild peppermint flavour.

Method

  1. Weigh: Weigh all ingredients accurately.
  2. Combine: Add the kaolin clay, sage powder and peppermint powder to a completely dry bowl.
  3. Mix: Mix very thoroughly until the herbs are evenly distributed throughout the kaolin.
  4. Transfer: Transfer to a clean, completely dry airtight container.
  5. Keep dry: Keep the powder dry during storage.

Storage & Use

  1. Wet: Wet a soft toothbrush.
  2. Apply: Place a small amount of the powder onto the toothbrush.
  3. Brush gently: Brush the teeth gently for approximately 30–60 seconds.
  4. Rinse: Rinse the mouth thoroughly with water.
  5. Frequency: Use approximately 1–2 times per week for surface-stain removal.

Use only superfine kaolin suitable for oral/food use and very finely powdered herbs.

Comedogenic Considerations

  • Not applicable to its intended oral-care use.

Notes

  • Contains no baking soda, oil, charcoal, peroxide or acids.
  • This recipe is currently grouped under Testing.
