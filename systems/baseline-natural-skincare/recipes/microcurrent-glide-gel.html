Microcurrent Glide Gel

Water-based conductive gel with sustained glide for microcurrent treatment.

Recipe Ingredient Information

Ingredients

  • 35.36 g Distilled Water
  • 4.00 g Vegetable Glycerin
  • 0.04 g Dead Sea Salt
  • 0.60 g Guar Gum

Total – 40.00 g

Method

  1. Disperse: Add the guar gum to the vegetable glycerin and mix thoroughly until evenly dispersed.
  2. Dissolve: Dissolve the Dead Sea salt completely in the distilled water.
  3. Combine: Slowly add the salt water to the glycerin and guar mixture while stirring continuously.
  4. Mix: Mix thoroughly until smooth and evenly combined.
  5. Hydrate: Allow the guar gum to hydrate fully and develop its final gel consistency.
  6. Stir: Stir again once fully hydrated.
  7. Portion: Transfer the amount needed for use into a clean, dry ramekin or other easily washable container.

Storage & Use

  1. Apply: Apply a generous layer of gel to the area being treated.
  2. Treat: Use the microcurrent device over the gel while it remains wet and slippery.
  3. Reapply: Add more gel if the device begins to drag or the gel starts drying.
  4. Finish: Rinse or wipe the gel away after the device treatment.
  5. Clean: Wash and fully dry the ramekin after use before using it for another batch.

Comedogenic Considerations

  • The formula contains no oils. Individual irritation or sensitivity can still occur.

Notes

  • The formula totals exactly 40.00 g.
  • This is a water-based, unpreserved formula. Prepare it fresh or use an appropriate preservation system if it is stored.

Purpose

A water-based conductive working gel that maintains wet contact and sustained glide during microcurrent treatment.

Formula Profile

Water-based gel • leave on during device treatment, then rinse or wipe away • vegan • 40 g batch • unpreserved / freshly prepared • thick gel • wet, high-glide finish.

How the Formulation Works

Water provides the conductive medium; glycerin extends wet glide; dissolved Dead Sea salt provides electrolytes; guar gives the gel enough stationary structure to remain underneath the device.

Design Rationale

Glycerin is kept high enough to extend working time without becoming the dominant phase. Guar provides stationary gel structure so the product remains underneath the device rather than running.

Variables & Fixed Elements

No slider or variant. The conductive medium, humectant, electrolyte and thickener proportions are fixed in the reference formula.

Boundaries / What It Is Not Designed To Do

Designed primarily as a conductive working medium for microcurrent treatment rather than as a standalone moisturiser. Because it is water-based and unpreserved, prepare it fresh or use an appropriate preservation system if storing it.
