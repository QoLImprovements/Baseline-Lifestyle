Dried Mixed Herbs

TypeBlend of dried culinary herbs

Mixed herbs are a general-purpose blend rather than a single ingredient, commonly combining herbs such as oregano, thyme, basil, marjoram, parsley or rosemary. Exact composition varies by brand.

Nutrition & composition

  • Herbs contain many plant compounds and micronutrients by weight, but culinary servings are small.
  • No single nutrient profile applies because the blend composition varies.
  • The principal contribution is flavour complexity rather than macronutrients.

Culinary characteristics

  • Designed to provide a balanced herb background without one herb dominating.
  • Dried herbs need moisture and time to rehydrate and release flavour.
  • Works particularly well in long-cooked soups, stews and sauces.

Uses & preparation

  • Soups, stews, sauces and casseroles.
  • Roasted vegetables, pulses and grain dishes.
  • Seasoning blends, marinades and savoury baking.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Check the ingredient list if avoiding a particular herb or added salt.
  • Store airtight, dry and away from light and heat.
  • Replace when aroma has faded substantially.
