Yellow Pepper

TypeRipe sweet pepper fruit (Capsicum annuum)

Yellow peppers are ripe sweet peppers with a crisp texture, bright colour and sweetness between typical green and red peppers. They are useful raw or cooked without a strongly bitter note.

Nutrition & composition

  • Rich in vitamin C and provides dietary fibre.
  • Contains yellow carotenoid pigments and other plant compounds associated with ripening.
  • High water content keeps energy density relatively low.

Culinary characteristics

  • Sweet and crisp raw; softens quickly when sautéed or roasted.
  • Retains a bright colour when lightly cooked, though long cooking dulls it.
  • Works in sauces without dominating them as strongly as some red pepper preparations.

Uses & preparation

  • Salads, sandwiches and raw vegetable plates.
  • Stir-fries, tray bakes and stuffed peppers.
  • Soups, stews, curries and blended sauces.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Choose firm peppers with taut skin and no soft patches.
  • Refrigerate and keep dry.
  • Seeds and white pith are edible but often removed for texture.
