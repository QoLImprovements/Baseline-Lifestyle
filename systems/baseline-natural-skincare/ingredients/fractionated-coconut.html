Fractionated Coconut Oil

🧪 INCI: Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride 🌱 Source part: Coconut oil fraction 🗺️ Geographic origin: Global 🧴 Comedogenic: 1 💚 Benefits: Very light spreadability; quick glide; clean finish 🔧 Usage: 2–60% • 🧊 Stability/Storage: 1–2 years Notes: Light, stable carrier that spreads quickly and reduces drag. Useful when you want a cleaner, faster-moving oil phase. Often used to offset richer oils without making the blend feel dry. Relevant recipes: Anhydrous Massage Oil Blend .
