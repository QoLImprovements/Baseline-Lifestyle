Herbes de Provence

TypeDried herb blend associated with Provençal cooking

Herbes de Provence is a variable dried blend usually built around herbs such as thyme, rosemary, savoury, oregano or marjoram; some blends also include lavender. There is no single universal formula.

Nutrition & composition

  • The blend contributes aromatic plant compounds and small amounts of micronutrients at culinary serving sizes.
  • Nutrient composition depends entirely on the herbs used.
  • Its primary role is flavour, allowing broad seasoning without added sugar or fat.

Culinary characteristics

  • Woody and Mediterranean-style herbs stand up well to long cooking.
  • Pairs especially well with tomato, pulses and roasted vegetables.
  • Lavender-containing blends can taste noticeably floral and should be dosed more cautiously.

Uses & preparation

  • Roasted vegetables and potatoes.
  • Soups, stews, sauces and pulses.
  • Marinades, tray bakes and savoury grain dishes.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Read the ingredient list because formulas vary widely.
  • Store airtight, dark and dry.
  • Use less initially with strongly flavoured blends and adjust after the herbs have hydrated.
