Raw 40°C Almond–Flax Pizza Base

Batch: 1×
Batch Multiplier
Measurement Type
Use:
Batch quantity: 1 • Measurement: Standard Variance

Purpose

A firm, sliceable dehydrated pizza base built from almonds and flaxseed for a whole-food, gluten-free alternative to refined flour crusts.

Recipe Profile

Dehydrator recipe • vegan • gluten-free • nut-and-seed based • higher-fibre, higher-fat base. Allergens: Tree nuts (almonds).

How the Recipe Works

Almonds provide body and richness; flaxseed binds water and helps the mixture hold together as it dehydrates. Nutritional yeast and herbs add restrained savoury depth.

Design Rationale

The recipe keeps the ingredient list compact and uses dehydration rather than baking so the base develops structure while retaining a dense whole-food character.

Variables & Fixed Elements

Thickness, dehydration time and final moisture level can be adjusted for a softer or firmer base. The almond/flax foundation is fixed.

Limitations & Trade-offs

This is denser and richer than a conventional flour crust. A thicker base needs longer dehydration, while taking it too dry can make it brittle.

Ingredients (View)

  • Base
  • 1 cup almonds
  • ¾ cup water
  • Seasoning
  • ½ cup Linseed/Flaxseed (Whole or Ground)
  • ¼ cup nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Prepare almonds: If using whole almonds, soak them in enough water to cover for 8–12 hours, then drain and rinse. Blend the almonds with the water into a coarse paste. If using ground almonds, mix the almonds with the water into a soft paste.
  2. Make dough: Stir in the linseed/flaxseed, nutritional yeast, dried parsley, dried basil, sea salt, and lemon juice. Rest for 10 minutes so the linseed/flaxseed can thicken the mixture.
  3. Shape: Spread the dough 5–7 mm thick on a lined dehydrator tray. Score lightly if you want easier portions later.
  4. Dehydrate: Dehydrate at 40°C for 6 hours, flip, then continue 8–10 hours until the base is firm yet flexible.
  5. Store: Cool fully before storing.

Serving & Use

  • Spread with a mild tomato base + herbs
  • Top with mashed avocado + lemon
  • Serve with steamed greens on the side

Body & Sensory Notes

  • Odour profile: Generally low‑odour ingredients; lemon and herbs tend to keep the profile fresh.
  • Dose notes: At larger servings, nuts/flax can feel heavier for some—start smaller, chew well, and increase gradually if needed.
  • Energy: Higher‑fat, higher‑fibre; typically steadier energy than refined carbs.
