Creamy Stovetop Polenta

Batch: 1×
Batch Multiplier
Measurement Type
Use:
Batch quantity: 1 • Measurement: Standard Variance

Purpose

A soft, creamy polenta bowl with tahini and nutritional yeast for richness, finished with avocado and lemon.

Recipe Profile

Stovetop recipe • vegan • gluten-free when ingredients are verified • soft, spoonable texture. Allergens: Sesame (tahini).

How the Recipe Works

Polenta supplies the starch base; water controls softness; tahini adds fat and creaminess; nutritional yeast adds savoury depth; avocado and lemon provide a fresh, rich topping.

Design Rationale

The recipe is deliberately mild and low-aromatic, using texture and gentle savoury ingredients rather than strong seasoning.

Variables & Fixed Elements

Water is the main texture variable: less gives firmer polenta and more gives a softer bowl. Batch size and measurement display can also be scaled.

Limitations & Trade-offs

Polenta thickens quickly as it stands and may need extra water when reheated. Larger servings are carbohydrate-forward and the tahini/avocado finish makes the dish richer.

Ingredients (View)

  • Base
  • ½ cup polenta
  • 1 tsp Herbes de Provence
  • ¼ tsp sea salt
  • Creamy finish
  • 2 tbsp roasted unhulled tahini
  • ¼ cup nutritional yeast
  • 1½–2 cups water
  • Avocado topping
  • 2 avocados
  • 13 g lemon juice
  • ⅛ tsp sea salt

Method

  1. Mix dry base: In a saucepan, whisk together the polenta, Herbes de Provence, and sea salt.
  2. Cook: Add the water and tahini. Bring to a gentle simmer over low–medium heat, stirring or whisking frequently until thick and creamy (about 8–12 minutes, depending on grind).
  3. Adjust texture: For softer polenta, add small splashes of water and stir until smooth and glossy.
  4. Finish off-heat: Remove from heat and stir in the nutritional yeast until fully incorporated.
  5. Top: Mash the avocados with the lemon juice and sea salt. Spoon over the polenta and serve immediately.

Serving & Use

  • Serve soft as a bowl, or chill and slice into squares
  • Top with avocado + lemon
  • Pair with steamed greens or a plain tomato side

Body & Sensory Notes

  • Odour profile: Mild, neutral base; pairs well with fresh herbs without strong aromatics.
  • Dose notes: At very large servings this can feel heavier for some—start smaller and balance with vegetables/protein for best tolerance.
  • Energy: Carb‑forward; can be quick energy—add fats/protein for steadier release.
