Red Pepper

TypeRipe sweet pepper fruit (Capsicum annuum)

Red peppers are fully ripened sweet peppers with a crisp raw texture and pronounced sweetness. Ripening changes both flavour and pigment profile compared with green peppers.

Nutrition & composition

  • Particularly rich in vitamin C and also provides fibre.
  • Contains carotenoid pigments, including red and orange compounds that increase with ripening.
  • High water content keeps energy density relatively low.

Culinary characteristics

  • Sweet and crisp raw; roasting intensifies sweetness and softens the flesh.
  • Blends into smooth sauces and soups after cooking.
  • Charred skins can be removed for a softer, cleaner-textured purée.

Uses & preparation

  • Salads, sandwiches and raw vegetable plates.
  • Roasted vegetables, soups, stews and curries.
  • Sauces, dips, stuffed peppers and tray bakes.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Choose firm, glossy peppers without soft or sunken areas.
  • Refrigerate and keep dry until use.
  • Seeds and pith are edible but commonly removed for texture.
