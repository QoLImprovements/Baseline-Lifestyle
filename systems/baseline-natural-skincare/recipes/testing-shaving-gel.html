Shaving Gel Recipe

Ingredients

  • Water – 240 ml
  • Slippery Elm Powder – 2 tablespoons
  • Vegetable Glycerin – 5 ml
  • Guar Gum – enough to thicken to preferred gel consistency
  • Jojoba Oil – 1.25 ml

Ingredient Roles

  • Slippery Elm Powder – provides strong mucilage, razor slip and cushioning.
  • Vegetable Glycerin – adds glide and helps retain moisture.
  • Guar Gum – creates the gel structure and controls thickness.
  • Jojoba Oil – provides lubrication and leaves a light protective emollient layer on the skin.
  • Water – hydrates the slippery elm and guar gum.

Method

  1. Combine: Add the slippery elm powder to the water and mix thoroughly.
  2. Hydrate: Allow it to hydrate and develop its natural mucilage.
  3. Add glycerin: Mix in the vegetable glycerin.
  4. Thicken: Gradually add guar gum until the desired shaving-gel thickness is reached.
  5. Finish: Mix in the jojoba oil thoroughly.
  6. Store appropriately: Use a suitable preservative if keeping the water-based gel for more than immediate use.

Storage & Use

  • Use as a shaving gel on wet skin, applying enough to maintain slip and cushioning during shaving.
  • This is a water-based testing formula. If it is kept for more than immediate use, use a suitable preservation system and hygienic storage.

Comedogenic Considerations

  • The formula is predominantly water-based and contains only a small amount of jojoba oil. Individual skin responses vary; patch test if you are particularly congestion-prone.

Notes

  • Guar gum is intentionally left variable so the final thickness can be adjusted to the preferred shaving-gel consistency.
