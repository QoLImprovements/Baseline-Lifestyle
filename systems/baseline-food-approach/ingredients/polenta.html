Polenta

TypeGround maize / cornmeal used for polenta

Polenta is ground maize cooked with liquid into a soft porridge or allowed to set into a sliceable form. Grind size ranges from fine to coarse and has a major effect on texture and cooking time.

Nutrition & composition

  • Predominantly carbohydrate, with some protein and fibre depending on how refined the meal is.
  • Yellow maize products provide carotenoid pigments; exact levels vary by maize variety.
  • Lower in protein and fibre than many whole pulses or intact whole grains.

Culinary characteristics

  • Starch absorbs hot liquid and produces a creamy or firm-set structure.
  • Coarse polenta has more texture; fine or instant products cook faster and smoother.
  • Can be served soft, chilled and sliced, baked or grilled.

Uses & preparation

  • Soft porridge-style side dishes.
  • Set slices, baked squares and grilled pieces.
  • Bases for vegetables, pulses, mushrooms and sauces.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Follow the grind-specific cooking instructions because instant and traditional polenta behave differently.
  • Whisking during early cooking helps reduce lumps.
  • Maize is naturally gluten-free, but choose appropriately labelled products where cross-contamination matters.
