Oat Groats

TypeWhole, minimally processed oat kernels (Avena sativa)

Oat groats are the intact dehulled oat kernel and the least processed common form of whole oats. They cook into a chewy grain rather than a flaked porridge.

Nutrition & composition

  • Wholegrain source of carbohydrate, fibre and plant protein.
  • Contains oat beta-glucan soluble fibre.
  • Provides minerals including magnesium, manganese, phosphorus and iron.

Culinary characteristics

  • Chewy texture and distinct kernels remain after cooking.
  • Longer cooking time than rolled oats, with a grain-like rather than porridge-like result.
  • Can absorb savoury broths and seasonings similarly to other whole grains.

Uses & preparation

  • Whole-grain bowls and salads.
  • Risotto-style and pilaf-style dishes.
  • Breakfast grain bowls and batch cooking.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Soaking can shorten cooking time but does not eliminate the need for cooking.
  • Store airtight, cool and dry.
  • Choose specifically gluten-free oats when gluten cross-contamination must be avoided.
