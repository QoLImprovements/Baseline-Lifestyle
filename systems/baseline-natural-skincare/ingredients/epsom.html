Epsom Salt

🧪 INCI: Magnesium Sulfate 🌱 Source part: Mineral salts 🗺️ Geographic origin: Mined/processed globally 🧴 Comedogenic: 0 💚 Benefits: Bath soaks; relaxing mineral soak feel; rinse-off exfoliation 🔧 Usage: Bath soaks as desired • 🧊 Stability/Storage: Indefinite if dry Notes: Magnesium sulfate crystals used primarily for bath soaks. Good for: relaxing soaks and a “mineral bath” feel (not for leave‑on skincare). Use: dissolve fully; avoid broken/irritated skin if it stings.
