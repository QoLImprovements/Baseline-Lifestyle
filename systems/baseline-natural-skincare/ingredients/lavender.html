Lavender Essential Oil

🧪 INCI: Lavandula Angustifolia Oil 🌱 Source part: Flowering tops ( steam distillate ) 🗺️ Geographic origin: France, Bulgaria, UK, Spain 🧴 Comedogenic: 0 💚 Benefits: Soothing aroma; mild antimicrobial (very low dose) 🔧 Usage: 0.1–0.5% leave-on • 🧊 Storage: cool, dark; cap tightly Notes: Fragrant essential oil used for scent + gentle soothing in low doses. Good for: bedtime balms and calming blends (keep total EO load low). Sensitivity note: patch test; avoid on broken/irritated skin.
