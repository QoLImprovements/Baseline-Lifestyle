Carnauba Wax

🧪 INCI: Copernicia Cerifera Wax 🌱 Source part: Palm leaf exudate 🗺️ Geographic origin: Northeastern Brazil 🧴 Comedogenic: 1 💚 Benefits: Film-forming protection; structure and gloss 🔧 Usage: 2–20% • 🧊 Stability/Storage: 3+ years Notes: High‑melting vegan wax that adds firmness and heat resistance. Good for: solid balms, deodorant sticks, firmer lip products. Can feel draggy alone—balance with butters/oils. Relevant recipes: Protective Touch Balm .
