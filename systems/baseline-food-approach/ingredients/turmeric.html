Turmeric

🌱 Type: Ground spice root (Curcuma longa) 💚 Benefits: Strong colour in tiny amounts; useful for warm yellow tone. 👃 Odour note: Earthy spice aroma; best kept light if you want a clean result. Notes: Turmeric is mainly useful here for colour, not as a dominant flavour. Good for: tiny colour adjustments in savoury blends, soups, and sauce bases.
