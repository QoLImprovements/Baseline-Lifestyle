Watermelon Seed Oil

🧪 INCI: Citrullus Lanatus Seed Oil 🌱 Source part: Watermelon seed 🗺️ Geographic origin: Africa, Mediterranean, India (varies by supplier) 🧴 Comedogenic: ~1 (generally low) 💚 Benefits: Lightweight, quick-absorbing; linoleic-acid rich; supports softness without heaviness 🔧 Usage: 2–30% • 🧊 Stability/Storage: ~1 year (cool/dark; refrigerate to extend) Notes: Very lightweight seed oil; typically linoleic‑forward with a dry finish. Good for: acne‑prone or congested skin where heavier oils feel occlusive. Use for: facial oils and lightweight body serums.
