Avocado

TypeFat-rich fruit (Persea americana)

Avocado is a creamy fruit distinguished from most fruit by its high fat content. Its mild flavour and soft texture make it useful as a spread, mash, garnish and blending ingredient.

Nutrition & composition

  • Provides predominantly unsaturated fat, along with fibre.
  • Contributes folate, potassium and vitamins including E and K.
  • Energy density is higher than most fruits because of the fat content.

Culinary characteristics

  • Ripe flesh is creamy and emulsifies easily into dressings, dips and sauces.
  • Mild flavour works with savoury, acidic and spicy ingredients.
  • Browns after cutting as the flesh oxidises.

Uses & preparation

  • Toast, salads, bowls and sandwiches.
  • Guacamole, dips, dressings and cold sauces.
  • Smoothies or blended preparations where creaminess is wanted.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Ripen at room temperature, then refrigerate ripe fruit to slow further softening.
  • Acid and reduced air exposure help slow browning after cutting.
  • Discard fruit with extensive rancid, fermented or mouldy areas.
