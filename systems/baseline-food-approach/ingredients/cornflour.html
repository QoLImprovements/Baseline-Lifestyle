Cornflour

TypeRefined maize starch (cornstarch in US terminology)

UK cornflour is a fine purified starch from maize. It is used mainly for its predictable thickening behaviour rather than for protein, fibre or micronutrient content.

Nutrition & composition

  • Almost entirely carbohydrate in the form of starch.
  • Contains very little protein, fat or fibre compared with whole maize.
  • Not nutritionally equivalent to whole cornmeal or polenta.

Culinary characteristics

  • Disperses best in cold liquid before being heated.
  • Starch granules swell during heating and create a smooth thickened sauce.
  • Too much heat or prolonged aggressive cooking can alter final thickness.

Uses & preparation

  • Sauces, gravies, soups and dessert fillings.
  • Coatings and batters.
  • Gluten-free baking blends where a light starch is useful.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Keep completely dry and sealed to prevent clumping.
  • Mix into a cold slurry before adding to hot liquid to reduce lumps.
  • Maize is naturally gluten-free, but labelling matters where cross-contamination must be excluded.
