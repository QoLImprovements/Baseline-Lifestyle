Sweet Potato & Bell Pepper Sauce

Batch: 1×
Batch Multiplier
Measurement Type
Use:
Batch quantity: 1 • Measurement: Standard Variance

Purpose

A smooth, mildly sweet vegetable-and-cashew sauce for chips, pasta, potatoes or roasted vegetables.

Recipe Profile

Stovetop/blended recipe • vegan • gluten-free • smooth, creamy vegetable sauce. Smooth blended sauce. Equipment: Soup maker + blender, or hob + blender. Allergens: Tree nuts (cashews).

How the Recipe Works

Sweet potato gives starch, sweetness and body; red pepper adds freshness; cashews provide creaminess; water controls flow; herbs, lemon and salt finish the flavour.

Design Rationale

The sauce uses root vegetable and nuts to create creaminess without dairy, extracted oil or a heavily aromatic seasoning base.

Variables & Fixed Elements

Water controls pourability and blending time controls smoothness. Lemon and seasoning can be adjusted lightly; the sweet-potato, pepper and cashew structure stays fixed.

Limitations & Trade-offs

Too little liquid makes the sauce heavy, while too much dilutes the creamy texture. Sweet potato makes this naturally sweeter and denser than a tomato-led sauce.

Ingredients (View)

  • Base
  • 500 g Sweet Potato
  • 300 g Red Pepper
  • 70 g Cashews or 2½ tbsp Cashew Butter
  • 300 ml Water
  • ½ tsp Dried Mixed Herbs
  • Finish
  • 2 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • Sea Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Option 1 — Soup maker + blender: Add the Sweet Potato, Red Pepper, Cashews or Cashew Butter, Water, and Dried Mixed Herbs. Run the smooth soup cycle. Add the Lemon Juice and Sea Salt, then blend briefly again until fully smooth.
  2. Option 2 — Hob: Add the Sweet Potato, Red Pepper, Cashews or Cashew Butter, Water, and Dried Mixed Herbs to a saucepan. Heat gently for 3–5 minutes, just until hot and the cashews are softened if using them.
  3. Blend + finish: Blend until smooth. Add the Lemon Juice and Sea Salt, blend or stir again, then use for chips or pasta.

Serving & Use

  • Use for chips
  • Stir through hot pasta
  • Spoon over baked potato or roasted vegetables

Body & Sensory Notes

  • Odour profile: Mild and softly sweet; not heavily aromatic.
  • Texture: Smooth, creamy sauce rather than a dip.
  • Energy: Root vegetable + nut base gives a steadier feel than a thin tomato-only sauce.
