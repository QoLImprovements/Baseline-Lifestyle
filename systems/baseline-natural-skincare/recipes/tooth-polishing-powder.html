Tooth Polishing Powder

Kaolin, sage and peppermint powder for occasional surface-stain removal.

Ingredients

  • 24 g Superfine Food/Pharmaceutical-Grade White Kaolin Clay
  • 4.5 g Very Finely Powdered Sage Leaf
  • 1.5 g Very Finely Powdered Peppermint Leaf

Total – 30 g

Method

  1. Weigh: Weigh all ingredients accurately.
  2. Combine: Add the kaolin clay, sage powder and peppermint powder to a completely dry bowl.
  3. Mix: Mix very thoroughly until the herbs are evenly distributed throughout the kaolin.
  4. Transfer: Transfer to a clean, completely dry airtight container.
  5. Keep dry: Keep the powder dry during storage.

Storage & Use

  1. Wet: Wet a soft toothbrush.
  2. Apply: Place a small amount of the powder onto the toothbrush.
  3. Brush gently: Brush the teeth gently for approximately 30–60 seconds.
  4. Rinse: Rinse the mouth thoroughly with water.
  5. Frequency: Use approximately 1–2 times per week for occasional surface-stain removal.

Comedogenic Considerations

  • Not applicable to the intended oral-care use. This is not a leave-on facial formula.

Notes

  • Use only superfine kaolin suitable for oral/food use and very finely powdered herbs.
  • Contains no baking soda, oil, charcoal, peroxide or acids.

Purpose

A dry tooth-polishing powder for occasional removal of surface staining without peroxide, acids, charcoal or baking soda.

Formula Profile

Dry powder • oral-care use • fixed 30 g batch • 80% kaolin • 15% sage • 5% peppermint.

How the Formulation Works

Superfine white kaolin provides the polishing phase; very finely powdered sage provides the supporting herb phase; peppermint provides mild flavour and freshness.

Design Rationale

The blend is deliberately short and dry, with the polishing function coming from superfine kaolin rather than a chemical bleaching agent.

Variables & Fixed Elements

No slider or variant. The formula is fixed at 24 g kaolin, 4.5 g sage and 1.5 g peppermint for a 30 g batch.

Boundaries / What It Is Not Designed To Do

Designed for occasional surface-stain removal. It is not a bleaching or peroxide whitening treatment.
