Tahini

TypePaste made from ground sesame seeds

Tahini is a dense sesame paste with a creamy texture and a distinct roasted, slightly bitter flavour. It is used both as a primary ingredient and as a base for emulsified sauces and dressings.

Nutrition & composition

  • Provides unsaturated fat, plant protein and fibre.
  • Contributes minerals including copper, calcium, iron and magnesium; calcium content varies with seed processing and whether hulls are retained.
  • Energy-dense because sesame seeds are naturally high in fat.

Culinary characteristics

  • Thickens dramatically when first mixed with water, then loosens into a smooth emulsion as more liquid is incorporated.
  • Bitterness varies by roasting level, seed quality and brand.
  • Pairs especially well with lemon, pulses, vegetables and warm spices.

Uses & preparation

  • Hummus and other dips.
  • Dressings, sauces and drizzles.
  • Baking, desserts and savoury spreads.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Natural oil separation is normal; stir thoroughly.
  • Keep sealed and follow label storage advice after opening.
  • Sesame is one of the UK's regulated major allergens.
