Microcurrent Conductive Gel Recipe – 40 g

Ingredients

  • Distilled Water – 35.36 g
  • Vegetable Glycerin – 4.00 g
  • Dead Sea Salt – 0.04 g
  • Guar Gum – 0.60 g

Total – 40.00 g

Ingredient Roles

  • Distilled Water – provides the main water-based conductive medium.
  • Vegetable Glycerin – helps retain moisture, improves glide and slows the gel from drying during microcurrent use.
  • Dead Sea Salt – provides dissolved minerals and electrolytes to support conductivity.
  • Guar Gum – creates a thick, scoopable gel consistency and helps the product remain in place on the skin.

Method

  1. Disperse the guar: Add the guar gum to the vegetable glycerin and mix thoroughly until the guar is evenly dispersed.
  2. Dissolve the salt: Dissolve the Dead Sea salt completely in the distilled water.
  3. Combine slowly: Slowly add the salt water to the glycerin and guar gum mixture while stirring continuously.
  4. Mix: Mix thoroughly until smooth and evenly combined.
  5. Hydrate: Allow the guar gum to hydrate fully and develop its final gel consistency.
  6. Finish: Stir again once fully hydrated.
  7. Portion: Transfer the amount needed for use into a clean, dry ramekin or other easily washable container.

Storage & Use

  1. Apply: Apply a generous layer of gel to the area being treated.
  2. Treat: Use the microcurrent device over the gel while it remains wet and slippery.
  3. Reapply: Add more gel if the device begins to drag or the gel starts drying.
  4. Clean: Wash and fully dry the ramekin after use before using it for another batch.

This is a water-based testing formula. If it is stored rather than prepared for immediate use, use an appropriate preservation system and hygienic storage.

Comedogenic Considerations

  • The formula contains no oils. Pore-clogging risk from the base ingredients is expected to be low, although individual irritation or sensitivity can still occur.

Notes

  • The formula totals exactly 40.00 g.
  • Keep the gel sufficiently wet during treatment to maintain glide and avoid device drag.
