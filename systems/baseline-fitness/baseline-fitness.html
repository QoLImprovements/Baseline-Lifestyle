Why Calisthenics (Bodyweight)

Baseline Fitness is built around bodyweight training because it is a low-friction, repeatable system: minimal equipment, scalable difficulty, and clean feedback loops.

  • High compliance: train anywhere (home / park / travel).
  • Clear scaling: regressions + progressions keep effort appropriate.
  • Joint-friendly control: tempo + range of motion are adjustable.
  • Measurable baseline: a consistent final set (reps or hold time) gives you a stable reference point.
  • Stabiliser muscle usage: body-line control recruits stabilisers around shoulders, hips, trunk, and ankles.
  • Minor muscle activation: small supporting muscles contribute meaningfully when you control tempo and range.
  • Functional carryover: strength expressed through full-body coordination transfers well to daily movement.
  • Low overhead: fewer variables → clearer progression.

The aim is stability that compounds. Same movements, tracked consistently.
