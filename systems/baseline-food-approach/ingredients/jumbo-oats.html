Jumbo Oats

TypeLarge-flake rolled whole oats (Avena sativa)

Jumbo oats are whole oat groats steamed and rolled into relatively large flakes. They retain a distinct chewy texture and take longer to soften than finer porridge oats.

Nutrition & composition

  • Wholegrain source of carbohydrate, fibre and plant protein.
  • Contains beta-glucan, a soluble fibre characteristic of oats.
  • Also contributes minerals such as magnesium, manganese, phosphorus and iron.

Culinary characteristics

  • Large flakes retain more texture during long cooking and soaking.
  • Absorb liquid steadily and create creamy porridge while remaining visibly oat-like.
  • Useful in baked mixtures where distinct flakes are desirable.

Uses & preparation

  • Porridge and overnight oats.
  • Granola, muesli and baked oat dishes.
  • Cookies, flapjack-style baking and toppings.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Store dry and airtight; wholegrain fats mean very long or warm storage can produce stale flavours.
  • Oats are frequently cross-contaminated with gluten-containing cereals; use specifically gluten-free oats when required.
  • Cooking time and liquid absorption vary with flake thickness.
