Dead Sea Salt

🧪 INCI: Maris Sal (Dead Sea Salt) 🌱 Source part: Mineral salts 🗺️ Geographic origin: Dead Sea (Israel/Jordan) 🧴 Comedogenic: 0 💚 Benefits: Mineral-rich bath soaks; distinctive mineral profile; rinse-off scrubs 🔧 Usage: Bath soaks as desired • 🧊 Stability/Storage: Indefinite if dry Notes: Sea-mineral salt (Maris Sal) with a distinctive mineral profile and higher magnesium/potassium content than typical table salt. Good for: mineral bath soaks and scrubs (rinse off). Use: dissolve fully; patch test if sensitive; keep dry to prevent clumping.
