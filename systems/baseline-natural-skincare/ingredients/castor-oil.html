Castor Oil

🧪 INCI: Ricinus Communis Seed Oil 🌱 Source part: Castor seed 🗺️ Geographic origin: India, Brazil, China 🧴 Comedogenic: 1 💚 Benefits: Gentle cleansing pull; viscosity adds grip for oil washing 🔧 Usage: 2–20% • 🧊 Stability/Storage: 1–2 years Notes: Very viscous oil (ricinoleic‑acid rich) that adds cling and gloss. Good for: lip products, hair ends, brows/lashes (sparingly). Use low–moderate % to avoid tackiness. Relevant recipes: Anhydrous Oil Cleanser , Protective Touch Balm , Anhydrous Massage Oil Blend .
