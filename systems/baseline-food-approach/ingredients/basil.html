Basil (Dried)

TypeDried culinary herb (Ocimum basilicum)

Dried basil is a concentrated aromatic herb with sweet, peppery and lightly anise-like notes. It is primarily a flavouring ingredient rather than a major source of energy or macronutrients at normal culinary amounts.

Nutrition & composition

  • Dried herbs contain concentrated plant compounds and micronutrients by weight, but typical serving sizes are small.
  • Basil contains aromatic compounds responsible for its characteristic flavour.
  • Its nutritional contribution is usually secondary to its role in seasoning food.

Culinary characteristics

  • Best suited to dishes where the herb has time to rehydrate and disperse.
  • Flavour is softer and less fresh than fresh basil but more shelf-stable.
  • Pairs naturally with tomato, vegetables, pulses and Mediterranean-style seasoning.

Uses & preparation

  • Tomato sauces, soups, stews and casseroles.
  • Herb blends, roasted vegetables and pulses.
  • Dressings and savoury grain dishes.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Store airtight, dry and away from heat and direct light.
  • Replace when aroma has faded substantially; dried herbs lose potency long before they become unsafe.
  • Use by taste because dried basil strength varies with age and brand.
