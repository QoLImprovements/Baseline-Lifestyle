Sweetcorn

TypeSweet maize kernels (Zea mays)

Sweetcorn is maize harvested while the kernels are still tender and naturally sweet. It provides more starch and carbohydrate than many green vegetables while retaining a juicy, crisp-to-tender texture.

Nutrition & composition

  • Provides carbohydrate, dietary fibre and a modest amount of plant protein.
  • Contributes folate and several B vitamins, with mineral amounts varying by form.
  • Yellow sweetcorn contains carotenoid pigments including lutein and zeaxanthin.

Culinary characteristics

  • Naturally sweet kernels stay distinct rather than dissolving into a sauce.
  • Fresh, frozen and canned forms are all useful; added salt or sugar depends on the product.
  • Short cooking preserves a firmer bite, while longer cooking integrates it into soups and stews.

Uses & preparation

  • Soups, stews, hotpots and chowder-style dishes.
  • Salads, grain bowls, salsas and mixed vegetables.
  • Fritters, fillings, rice dishes and tray bakes.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Frozen sweetcorn can usually be cooked directly from frozen.
  • For canned sweetcorn, check the label if limiting added salt or sugar.
  • Store frozen kernels continuously frozen and reseal after opening.
