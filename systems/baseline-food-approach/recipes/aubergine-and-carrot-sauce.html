Aubergine & Carrot Sauce

Batch: 1×
Batch Multiplier
Measurement Type
Use:
Batch quantity: 1 • Measurement: Standard Variance

Purpose

A soft, blended vegetable sauce built around aubergine, carrot and cashew for a mellow pasta or potato topping.

Recipe Profile

Stovetop/blended recipe • vegan • gluten-free • smooth, low-aromatic sauce. Smooth blended pasta sauce. Equipment: Soup maker + blender, or hob + blender. Allergens: Tree nuts (cashews).

How the Recipe Works

Aubergine provides softness and silkiness; carrot adds gentle sweetness and body; cashews create creaminess; parsley, lemon and salt give a restrained finish.

Design Rationale

The recipe is designed to stay mild and smooth without depending on tomato, onion, garlic or heavy spice for flavour.

Variables & Fixed Elements

Water and blending time control the final texture. Lemon and salt can be adjusted lightly; the aubergine-carrot-cashew foundation remains fixed.

Limitations & Trade-offs

Aubergine and carrot must be fully soft before blending or the sauce can remain grainy. Excess water can flatten both texture and flavour.

Ingredients (View)

  • Base
  • 300 g Aubergine
  • 100 g Carrot
  • 30 g Cashews or 1 tbsp Cashew Butter
  • 110 ml Water
  • ½ tsp Dried Parsley
  • Finish
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • Sea Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Option 1 — Soup maker + blender: Add the Aubergine, Carrot, Cashews or Cashew Butter, Water, and Dried Parsley. Blend until smooth and heated through. Add the Lemon Juice and Sea Salt, then blend or stir briefly again.
  2. Option 2 — Hob: Add the Aubergine, Carrot, Cashews or Cashew Butter, Water, and Dried Parsley to a saucepan. Heat gently for 3–5 minutes, just until hot and the cashews are softened if using them.
  3. Blend + pasta finish: Blend until smooth. Stir in the Lemon Juice and Sea Salt, then add to hot pasta with 40 ml pasta water.

Serving & Use

  • Toss through hot pasta
  • Use as a soft sauce for baked potato or wedges
  • Spoon over roasted vegetables

Body & Sensory Notes

  • Odour profile: Soft and low-aromatic; no sharp spices.
  • Texture: Smooth, mellow, and slightly silky from the aubergine.
  • Energy: Vegetable-led sauce with a small nut base for extra staying power.
