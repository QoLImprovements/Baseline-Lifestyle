Nutritional Yeast

TypeDeactivated food yeast, usually Saccharomyces cerevisiae

Nutritional yeast is an inactive yeast sold as flakes or powder with a savoury, nutty, cheese-like flavour. It is used as a seasoning and ingredient rather than as a leavening yeast.

Nutrition & composition

  • Typically provides protein and naturally occurring B vitamins.
  • Vitamin B12 is present only when the product is fortified; it should not be assumed from the ingredient name alone.
  • Fortification levels and sodium vary significantly between brands.

Culinary characteristics

  • Strong umami and savoury character can deepen sauces, soups and toppings.
  • Flakes dissolve into moist foods and can add some body.
  • Does not make dough rise because the yeast is inactive.

Uses & preparation

  • Savoury seasoning for vegetables, potatoes and popcorn-style snacks.
  • Sauces, soups, dips and vegan cheese-style preparations.
  • Sprinkled over pasta alternatives, grains and salads.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Check the label for fortification and sodium rather than assuming a standard nutrient profile.
  • Store dry and sealed to preserve flavour.
  • Use a gluten-free-labelled product where cross-contamination is a concern.
