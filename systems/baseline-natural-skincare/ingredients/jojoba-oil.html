Jojoba Oil

🧪 INCI: Simmondsia Chinensis Seed Oil 🌱 Source part: Jojoba seed (liquid wax ester) 🗺️ Geographic origin: USA (SW), Mexico, Argentina, Israel, Australia 🧴 Comedogenic: 0–2 💚 Benefits: Biomimetic sebum; regulates feel; improves slip 🔧 Usage: 1–50% • 🧊 Stability/Storage: 2–3 years (very stable) Notes: Liquid wax ester similar to human sebum; supports a balanced skin feel. Good for: oily/combination skin, scalp/beard blends, reducing “greasy” feel in oils. Very stable; helps improve shelf life of blends. Relevant recipes: Nurturing Glow Butter , Protective Touch Balm , Anhydrous Massage Oil Blend .
