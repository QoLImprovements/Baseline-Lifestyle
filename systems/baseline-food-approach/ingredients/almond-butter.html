Almond Butter

TypeNut butter made from ground almonds

Almond butter is a concentrated almond paste with a creamy texture and a mild, slightly sweet nut flavour. It works as both a food in its own right and a versatile fat-and-protein ingredient.

Nutrition & composition

  • Provides mainly unsaturated fat, alongside plant protein and fibre.
  • Naturally contributes vitamin E, magnesium and other minerals found in almonds.
  • Nutrition changes when products contain added oil, sugar or salt, so the ingredient list matters.

Culinary characteristics

  • Creamy and cohesive; loosens into sauces or dressings when mixed with water or another liquid.
  • Mild nut flavour works in both sweet and savoury preparations.
  • Adds richness, body and emulsifying power rather than functioning only as a thickener.

Uses & preparation

  • Spreads, porridge and breakfast bowls.
  • Dressings, dips, sauces and blended soups.
  • Baking, desserts, smoothies and energy-dense snacks.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Natural almond butter may separate; stirring redistributes the oil.
  • Store sealed away from heat and light and follow the manufacturer's refrigeration advice after opening.
  • Tree-nut allergen. For a simpler product, choose one made mainly or entirely from almonds.
