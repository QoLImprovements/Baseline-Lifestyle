Potato

🌱 Type: Root vegetable / tuber (Solanum tuberosum) 💚 Benefits: Smooth starchy body, useful for thicker blends and firmer chilled textures. 👃 Odour note: Mild and neutral once cooked. Notes: Potato gives blended sauces and cheese-style bases body without a heavy aroma. Good for: mash-style meals, thick sauces, and set vegan cheese bases.
