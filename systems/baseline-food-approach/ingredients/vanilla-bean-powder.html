Vanilla Bean Powder

TypeGround vanilla bean, usually from Vanilla planifolia or related species

Pure vanilla bean powder is finely ground dried vanilla pod. It provides concentrated vanilla aroma and visible bean particles without adding the liquid found in vanilla extract.

Nutrition & composition

  • Used in very small amounts, so its macronutrient and micronutrient contribution is minor.
  • Its value comes mainly from aromatic compounds, including vanillin and many secondary flavour molecules.
  • Some products sold as vanilla powder contain sugar, starch or flavour carriers, so labels should be checked.

Culinary characteristics

  • Adds warm, floral, sweet-associated aroma without actually being a sweetener.
  • Works particularly well in oats, fruit, baking and creamy preparations.
  • Because pure powder contains the whole ground pod, it can leave fine dark specks.

Uses & preparation

  • Porridge, oats and breakfast dishes.
  • Baking, desserts and fruit preparations.
  • Smoothies, drinks and nut-based creams.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Keep airtight, cool and dark to preserve aroma.
  • Check whether the product is pure ground vanilla or a blended vanilla-flavoured powder.
  • Use small quantities first because strength differs markedly between products.
