Porridge Oats

TypeRolled or cut whole oats prepared for quick porridge cooking

Porridge oats are whole oats processed into smaller or thinner pieces than jumbo flakes so they hydrate and soften more quickly. They produce a creamier, less chewy porridge.

Nutrition & composition

  • Wholegrain source of carbohydrate, fibre and plant protein.
  • Contains beta-glucan soluble fibre characteristic of oats.
  • Provides minerals including magnesium, manganese, phosphorus and iron.

Culinary characteristics

  • Smaller flakes absorb water rapidly and release more starch into porridge.
  • Creates a creamy texture with less distinct flake structure than jumbo oats.
  • Also useful as a binder or moisture-absorbing ingredient in baking.

Uses & preparation

  • Porridge and overnight oats.
  • Baking, oat bars and crumble-style toppings.
  • Blended or ground for oat-based batters and coatings.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Store dry and airtight.
  • Oats are a regulated gluten-containing cereal for UK allergen labelling because contamination with wheat, barley or rye is common; use certified gluten-free oats when needed.
  • Liquid requirement varies with flake size and desired porridge thickness.
