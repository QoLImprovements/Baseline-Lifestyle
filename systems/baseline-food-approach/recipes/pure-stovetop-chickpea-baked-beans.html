Pure Stovetop Chickpea Baked Beans

Batch: 1×
Batch Multiplier
Measurement Type
Use:
Batch quantity: 1 • Measurement: Standard Variance

Purpose

A tomato-based chickpea dish designed as a whole-food alternative to sweetened or additive-heavy baked beans.

Recipe Profile

Stovetop recipe • vegan • gluten-free • legume-based • fibre- and protein-containing. Allergens: Nuts (nut butter).

How the Recipe Works

Chickpeas provide the main body; tomato, carrot and mushroom build the sauce; herbs add restrained savoury flavour; nut butter rounds the texture and lemon lifts the finish.

Design Rationale

The recipe avoids added sugar and strong aromatics while keeping a familiar soft bean-and-sauce format using whole ingredients.

Variables & Fixed Elements

Sauce thickness can be adjusted with water and cooking time. Chickpea tenderness depends on soaking and cooking; the basic structure stays fixed.

Limitations & Trade-offs

Larger chickpea portions can increase digestive fermentation for some people. Thorough cooking improves texture, and reducing too far can make the sauce heavy.

Ingredients (View)

  • Chickpeas
  • 250 g Chickpeas Dried
  • Sauce
  • 4–5 ripe tomatoes
  • 1 medium carrot
  • 250 g white mushrooms (finely chopped)
  • 200 ml water
  • Seasoning
  • 2 tsp Herbes de Provence
  • ¼ tsp sage
  • ¾ tsp sea salt
  • Finish
  • ⅛–¼ cup Brazil Nut Butter (or nut butter of your choosing)
  • ½–1 tsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Soak chickpeas: Rinse the chickpeas. Soak them in plenty of water for 8 hours. Drain and rinse well.
  2. Pressure Cooker cook: Add the soaked chickpeas to the Pressure Cooker with fresh water to cover well. Cook on High Pressure for 15 minutes. Natural release, then drain.
  3. Sauce: Water-sauté the carrot and white mushrooms for 6–8 minutes. Add the tomatoes, 200 ml water, Herbes de Provence, sage, and sea salt. Simmer 25–30 minutes.
  4. Combine: Add the cooked chickpeas and simmer 10–15 minutes.
  5. Nut butter: Stir in the Brazil Nut Butter and simmer 10–15 minutes.
  6. Finish: Add the lemon juice. Rest covered 5 minutes, then serve.

Serving & Use

  • Over baked/mashed sweet potato
  • With steamed greens
  • With roasted potatoes or sweet potato wedges

Body & Sensory Notes

  • Odour profile: Mild seasoning; legumes are generally neutral but can increase fermentation-related odour for some.
  • Dose notes: Larger portions of chickpeas may increase gas; consider smaller servings and thorough cooking.
  • Energy: Fibre + protein support steadier energy and satiety.
