Tapioca Starch

TypeRefined starch extracted from cassava root

Tapioca starch is a nearly pure starch used for thickening, binding and creating elastic or glossy textures. It is different from whole cassava flour because fibre and most non-starch components have been removed.

Nutrition & composition

  • Almost entirely carbohydrate as starch.
  • Contains negligible protein, fat and fibre.
  • Used primarily for functional texture rather than as a major source of micronutrients.

Culinary characteristics

  • Thickens liquids and can create a glossy, cohesive texture.
  • Useful for elasticity and chew in some gluten-free mixtures.
  • Needs controlled hydration and heat; too much can make foods gummy.

Uses & preparation

  • Sauces, soups and fruit fillings.
  • Gluten-free baking and dough blends.
  • Puddings, chewy textures and binding applications.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Keep dry and airtight.
  • Disperse in cool liquid before adding to very hot food when using it as a slurry.
  • Naturally gluten-free, but choose appropriately labelled products where cross-contamination matters.
