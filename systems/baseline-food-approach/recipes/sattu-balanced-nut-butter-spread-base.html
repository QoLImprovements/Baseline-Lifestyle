Sattu-Balanced Nut Butter Spread Base

Batch: 1×
Batch Multiplier
Measurement Type
Use:
Batch quantity: 1 • Measurement: Standard Variance

Purpose

A thick, no-cook spread that balances nut butter with roasted gram flour and flaxseed.

Recipe Profile

No-cook recipe • vegan • gluten-free when ingredients are verified • thick, spreadable texture. Gluten-free, vegan, no-cook base. Allergens: Tree nuts (almond butter).

How the Recipe Works

Almond butter supplies fat and creaminess; sattu adds roasted legume protein and dry body; flaxseed adds fibre and binding; staged water addition controls spreadability.

Design Rationale

The recipe reduces reliance on pure nut butter by sharing the structure with sattu and flaxseed, producing a more balanced whole-food spread base.

Variables & Fixed Elements

Water is the main texture control and should be added gradually. Lemon and salt are finishing variables; the almond-butter, sattu and flax balance is fixed.

Limitations & Trade-offs

Adding water too quickly can make the spread loose, while too little leaves it dense and dry. The mixture thickens further as the dry ingredients hydrate.

Ingredients (View)

  • Base Mix
  • 150 g Almond Butter
  • 120 g Sattu (Roasted Gram Flour)
  • 15 g Linseed/Flaxseed (Whole or Ground)
  • 2–3 g Sea Salt
  • Liquids
  • 15 g Lemon Juice (optional)
  • 0–15 g Water (as needed)

Method

  1. Make the base: In a bowl, mix sattu, flaxseed, and salt until uniform. Stir into almond butter until fully combined and thick.
  2. Store: Transfer to a clean, dry, airtight jar. Keep the jar dry (no water added to the storage jar).
  3. Portion: Scoop 45 g of the base into a small bowl.
  4. Optional lemon: Add 15 g lemon juice and mix thoroughly.
  5. Hydrate to spreadable: Add water gradually up to 0–15 g, mixing until spreadable.
  6. Rest + remix: Wait 30–60 seconds, then remix (sattu and flax continue to hydrate and thicken slightly).

Serving & Use

  • Serve in the meal context that suits the recipe.

Body & Sensory Notes

  • Odour profile: Generally mild/neutral; lemon is optional for a clean finish.
  • Texture: Thick, creamy base; hydrates into a smooth spread with staged water addition.
  • Energy: Higher-fat plus fibre/protein; typically steadier than refined spreads.
