Cashews

TypeTree nut / seed of Anacardium occidentale

Cashews are curved, creamy-textured nuts with a mild sweet flavour. Their softness relative to many other nuts makes them particularly useful for soaking and blending.

Nutrition & composition

  • Provides unsaturated fat, plant protein and some fibre.
  • Contributes copper, magnesium, zinc and other minerals.
  • Energy-dense because of the fat content.

Culinary characteristics

  • Softer and less fibrous in texture than almonds; becomes very creamy when blended.
  • Roasting deepens sweetness and nuttiness.
  • Soaking softens them further for smooth sauces and fillings.

Uses & preparation

  • Snacking, cereals, salads and stir-fries.
  • Creamy blended sauces, dips and desserts.
  • Nut butters, baking and dairy-free style fillings.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Store airtight and cool to protect the oils from rancidity.
  • Commercial edible cashews have already been processed to remove the irritating shell components.
  • Tree-nut allergen.
