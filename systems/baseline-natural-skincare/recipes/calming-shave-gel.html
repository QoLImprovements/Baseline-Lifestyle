Calming Shave Gel

Recipe Ingredient Information

240 ml master batch. Use the cushion slider to set guar gum as a percentage of the batch without changing the base water, slippery elm, glycerin, or jojoba.

Cushion Level
1.0% guar gum
Looser slipThicker cushion
Overall Feel

A looser gel rinses faster and feels lighter. A higher guar percentage gives more cushion and blade protection, but can feel more draggy if pushed too high.

Current batch: 240 ml water phase • 2 tbsp slippery elm • 5 ml glycerin • 2.4 g guar • 1.25 ml jojoba.

Ingredients

  • Gel base
  • 240 ml Filtered or distilled water (warmed to 45–50 °C)
  • 2 tbsp (level) Slippery elm powder
  • Thickener dispersion
  • 5 ml Vegetable glycerin
  • 2.4 g Guar Gum Powder
  • Finish
  • 1.25 ml Jojoba oil

Method

  1. Warm the water to 45–50 °C (warm, not hot).
  2. Whisk in the slippery elm powder until smooth.
  3. Rest for 10 minutes to fully hydrate the mucilage.
  4. In a small cup, pre-disperse the guar gum into the vegetable glycerin (mix until smooth, no dry pockets).
  5. Whisk the glycerin/guar mix into the gel until evenly thickened.
  6. Stir in the jojoba oil and whisk again until the texture is fully even.

Storage & Use

  • Refrigerate; use within 3–5 days (no preservative). Make small fresh batches.
  • Apply to warm, wet skin. Shave with gentle passes. Rinse with warm water and follow with your oil cleanse / butter as needed.

Comedogenic Considerations

  • Mostly water-based with tiny lipids (jojoba 0–2). Low clogging tendency; patch test if very congestion-prone.

Notes

  • If it feels too thick or slightly draggy, dilute in the palm with a few drops of warm water before applying.
  • If it dries mid-pass, increase glycerin by ¼ tsp (small steps only).
  • Your common ⅓ batch lands around 0.8 g guar at the current default, which sits in the ¼ tsp or slightly more zone.
  • No separate glide slider is used here: cushion is the useful variable, while glide stays anchored by slippery elm, glycerin, and jojoba.
