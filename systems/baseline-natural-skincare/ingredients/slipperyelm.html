Slippery Elm Powder

🧪 INCI: Ulmus Rubra Bark Extract 🌱 Source part: Inner bark , dried & milled 🗺️ Geographic origin: Eastern North America 🧴 Comedogenic: 0 💚 Benefits: Soothing gel; blade slip; calms irritation 🔧 Usage: ~1–2 tbsp per cup water • 🧊 Stability/Storage: 1–2 years dry (airtight) Notes: Plant mucilage powder that makes a soothing, gel-like texture in water. Good for: short‑life masks and calming gels. Make fresh and discard if smell/texture changes.
