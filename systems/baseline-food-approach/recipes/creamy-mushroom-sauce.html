Creamy Mushroom Sauce

Batch: 1×
Batch Multiplier
Measurement Type
Use:
Batch quantity: 1 • Measurement: Standard Variance

Purpose

A dairy-free creamy mushroom sauce for pasta, potatoes, rice or vegetables.

Recipe Profile

Stovetop recipe • vegan • gluten-free when starches are verified • creamy, savoury finish. Butter-free, dairy-free. Allergens: Sesame (tahini).

How the Recipe Works

Mushrooms provide the savoury base; tahini gives body and fat; cornflour or cassava flour thickens the water phase; parsley and salt keep the seasoning restrained.

Design Rationale

The formula creates creaminess without dairy, butter or extracted oil while keeping the ingredient list short and the flavour relatively mild.

Variables & Fixed Elements

Water and starch determine thickness. More liquid makes a pourable sauce; more reduction or starch gives a thicker coating consistency.

Limitations & Trade-offs

Mushrooms become more aromatic as they cook, and excess starch can make the sauce pasty. Tahini also makes the sauce richer as the portion increases.

Ingredients (View)

  • Sauté stage
  • 500 g mushrooms, sliced
  • ⅛ tsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • 4 tbsp water
  • Sauce base
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 1 litre water
  • Thickening slurry
  • 4 tbsp cornflour or cassava flour
  • 8 tbsp cold water
  • Finish
  • ¼ tsp sea salt

Method

  1. Sauté mushrooms: Add the mushrooms to a wide pan over medium heat. Add 4 tbsp water. Stir in the sea salt and dried parsley. Sauté 5–7 minutes until the mushrooms are softened and reduced.
  2. Add tahini: Stir in the tahini and cook 1–2 minutes to coat the mushrooms.
  3. Simmer: Add 1 litre water and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Thicken: In a small bowl, whisk the cornflour or cassava flour with 8 tbsp cold water to make a slurry. Pour in slowly while stirring. Simmer 2–4 minutes until thickened.
  5. Finish: Add the sea salt and adjust to taste. Remove from heat once creamy.

Serving & Use

  • Use as a creamy pasta sauce (stir through hot pasta and loosen with hot water if needed).
  • Spoon over baked potatoes or roasted vegetables.
  • Serve with rice or gluten-free noodles.

Body & Sensory Notes

  • Odour profile: Earthy but not pungent; herbs help keep the aroma clean.
  • Dose notes: Larger amounts of mushrooms and nut butter can be richer and more aromatic for some.
  • Energy: Calorie-dense; fats can increase satiety and steadiness when portioned appropriately.
