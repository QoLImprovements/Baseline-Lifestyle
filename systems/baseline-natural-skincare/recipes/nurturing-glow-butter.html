Nurturing Glow Butter

Recipe Ingredient Information

Ingredients

  • Butter base
  • 420 g Shea Butter (Unrefined) (softened)
  • Oils
  • 60 ml Cucumber Seed Oil
  • 60 ml Argan Oil
  • 120 ml Jojoba Oil
  • Aromatic
  • 60 drops Lavender Essential Oil (≈0.5%)

Method

  1. Whip the softened shea butter until light and fluffy.
  2. Drizzle in the cucumber seed oil, argan oil, and jojoba oil slowly while whipping until fully incorporated.
  3. Chill for 10 minutes, then whip again for a stable, airy texture.
  4. Add the lavender essential oil and whip briefly to distribute evenly.
  5. Jar immediately. Tap the jar gently to release trapped air pockets, then cap.

Storage & Use

  • Store cool & dry; avoid direct sunlight/heat. Shelf life: 12–24 months.
  • Apply after shower/steam while skin is warm and slightly damp.

Comedogenic Considerations

  • Overall low–moderate depending on skin. Patch test if congestion-prone.

Notes

  • Soft-set at room temperature; richer and more cushiony than a firm balm, and it melts quickly on contact.
