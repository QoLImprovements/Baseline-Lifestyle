Mung Bean & Peanut Vegetable Hotpot

Batch: 1×
Batch Multiplier
Measurement Type
Use:
Batch quantity: 1 • Measurement: Standard Variance

Purpose

A substantial overnight slow-cooker hotpot built around mung beans, vegetables and peanut butter, with a tapioca finish for a cohesive sauce.

Recipe Profile

Slow-cooker recipe • vegan • base batch makes 8 portions • 8 hours on LOW plus 5–10 minutes after thickening. Allergens: Peanuts.

How the Recipe Works

Mung beans provide the legume base; potatoes and carrots give body; tomatoes, peppers, peas, sweetcorn and spinach build the vegetable volume; peanut butter adds richness; tapioca starch thickens the cooking liquid at the end.

Design Rationale

The recipe is designed as a large, repeatable batch meal: most ingredients cook together unattended, while the slurry is held back until the beans and vegetables are fully cooked.

Variables & Fixed Elements

Water level and final tapioca quantity control thickness. Tomato size varies naturally. The base timing remains 8 hours on LOW, with lemon added per portion at serving.

Limitations & Trade-offs

A very full slow cooker can take longer to heat through. Mung beans and potato pieces must be completely tender before serving; add the tapioca only after the main cook so it does not thicken the liquid prematurely.

Ingredients (View)

  • Main hotpot
  • 1 kg potatoes, chopped
  • 500 g carrots, chopped
  • 300 g dried mung beans, rinsed
  • 200 g frozen peas
  • 200 g frozen sweetcorn
  • 200 g frozen spinach
  • 8–10 medium fresh tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 100 g peanut butter
  • 4 tsp mixed herbs
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1.8 L water
  • Tapioca slurry
  • 40 g tapioca starch
  • 53 g cold water
  • To serve
  • Per portion: 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Extra salt, only if needed

Method

  1. Load the slow cooker: Add the potatoes, carrots, mung beans, peas, sweetcorn, spinach, tomatoes, peppers, peanut butter, mixed herbs, salt and water to the slow cooker.
  2. Combine: Stir well.
  3. Slow cook: Cook on LOW for 8 hours.
  4. Make the slurry: Mix the tapioca starch with the cold water until completely smooth.
  5. Thicken: Stir the slurry thoroughly into the hotpot.
  6. Finish: Cook for a further 5–10 minutes, until thickened.
  7. Serve: Add 1 tsp lemon juice per portion.
  8. Season: Taste before adding any extra salt.

Serving & Use

  • Base batch: 8 portions.
  • Add the lemon to each portion at serving rather than to the whole slow-cooker batch.
  • Reassess salt only after the lemon has been added.

Body & Sensory Notes

  • Texture: Thick, soft and substantial, with the tapioca slurry bringing the cooking liquid together at the end.
  • Readiness: Mung beans should be fully tender and potatoes should have no firm centre before the slurry is added.
  • Batch note: The vegetables settle substantially during the long slow cook, while the dried mung beans absorb part of the liquid.
