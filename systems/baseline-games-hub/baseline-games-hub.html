Playing

Drag pieces from the Main Tray or Sort trays onto the jigsaw. Correct pieces lock into place; a wrong drop leaves the piece available to try again. Drag pieces into Sort 1–4 to organise them, or back into the Main Tray.

Z
ZoomHover a tray piece and press Z to enlarge the entire jigsaw piece above the workspace while its tray card stays fixed. Hold Z over the jigsaw for the circular board magnifier; release Z to close it. Zoom works on the board and tray pieces while View is enabled.
