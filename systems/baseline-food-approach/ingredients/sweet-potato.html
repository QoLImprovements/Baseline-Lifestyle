Sweet Potato

TypeStarchy root (Ipomoea batatas)

Sweet potatoes are starchy roots with varieties ranging from dry and pale to moist, orange and strongly sweet. They are nutritionally and botanically distinct from ordinary potatoes.

Nutrition & composition

  • Provides carbohydrate and dietary fibre.
  • Orange-fleshed varieties are particularly rich in beta-carotene; other colours have different pigment profiles.
  • Also contributes potassium and vitamin C.

Culinary characteristics

  • Becomes soft and sweet with baking, roasting or steaming.
  • Orange varieties mash and blend very smoothly.
  • Natural sweetness suits both savoury and sweet seasoning.

Uses & preparation

  • Baked, roasted, steamed and mashed dishes.
  • Soups, curries, sauces and tray bakes.
  • Baking, patties and purées.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Store cool, dry and dark but not excessively cold.
  • Choose firm roots without soft decay.
  • Cooking method and variety strongly affect moisture and sweetness.
