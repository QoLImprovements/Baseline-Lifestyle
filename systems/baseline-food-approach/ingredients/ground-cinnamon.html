Ground Cinnamon

TypeGround bark spice from Cinnamomum species

Ground cinnamon is a warm, sweet-aromatic spice used in both sweet and savoury foods. Commercial cinnamon may be cassia-type cinnamon, Ceylon cinnamon, or a blend.

Nutrition & composition

  • Used in small amounts, so its practical contribution is mainly flavour and plant compounds rather than calories or macronutrients.
  • Contains aromatic polyphenols and volatile compounds characteristic of cinnamon.
  • Cassia cinnamon generally contains more coumarin than Ceylon cinnamon.

Culinary characteristics

  • Adds perceived sweetness without being a sugar.
  • Blooms readily in warm liquids and baked mixtures.
  • Works with fruit, oats, cocoa, coffee flavours and many savoury spice blends.

Uses & preparation

  • Porridge, baked fruit and desserts.
  • Baking, hot drinks and breakfast dishes.
  • Curries, stews and spice blends.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Store airtight away from heat and light; aroma fades with age.
  • Very large habitual amounts of cassia cinnamon are not equivalent to ordinary culinary seasoning because of coumarin exposure.
  • Use food-grade ground cinnamon rather than concentrated essential oil as a substitute.
