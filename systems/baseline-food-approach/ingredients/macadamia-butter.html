Macadamia Butter

TypeTree-nut butter made from macadamia nuts

Macadamia butter is an exceptionally smooth, rich nut paste with a mild buttery flavour. Its fat profile is dominated by monounsaturated fat and its texture is naturally fluid.

Nutrition & composition

  • High in fat, predominantly monounsaturated, and therefore energy-dense.
  • Provides some fibre and protein, though less protein than many other nut butters.
  • Contributes minerals and vitamin B1 (thiamin) found naturally in macadamias.

Culinary characteristics

  • Very creamy and mild, with less bitterness than many seed or nut pastes.
  • Adds richness without strongly dominating other flavours.
  • Can loosen readily because of its high oil content.

Uses & preparation

  • Spreads and porridge.
  • Desserts, fillings and smoothies.
  • Creamy sauces or dressings where a mild nut base is useful.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Natural separation is normal; stir before use.
  • Protect from heat and light to slow rancidity.
  • Tree-nut allergen.
