Mung Beans

TypePulse / legume (Vigna radiata)

Mung beans are small green pulses that cook more quickly than many larger dried beans. Whole beans hold some shape when gently cooked but can also break down into a soft, cohesive base.

Nutrition & composition

  • Provides plant protein, dietary fibre and starch.
  • Contributes folate, magnesium, potassium, iron and other micronutrients.
  • Low in saturated fat and naturally cholesterol-free.

Culinary characteristics

  • Mild earthy flavour works with both strongly and lightly seasoned dishes.
  • Can be cooked until separate and tender or longer until partially broken down.
  • Absorbs seasoning and cooking liquid effectively.

Uses & preparation

  • Soups, dals, curries and hotpots.
  • Grain-and-pulse bowls and salads when cooked to hold shape.
  • Sprouting uses a different food-safety process from cooking dried beans.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Rinse dried beans and cook until fully tender.
  • Store dried beans airtight and dry; very old pulses can take longer to soften.
  • If sprouting, follow dedicated sprout food-safety guidance rather than treating sprouts like cooked beans.
