Shaving Gel Recipe

Testing — formulation currently being tested before promotion into the main recipe collection.

Ingredients

  • 240 ml Water
  • 2 tablespoons Slippery Elm Powder
  • 5 ml Vegetable Glycerin
  • enough to thicken to preferred gel consistency Guar Gum
  • 1.25 ml Jojoba Oil

Ingredient Roles

  • Slippery Elm Powder – provides strong mucilage, razor slip and cushioning.
  • Vegetable Glycerin – adds glide and helps retain moisture.
  • Guar Gum – creates the gel structure and controls thickness.
  • Jojoba Oil – provides lubrication and leaves a light protective emollient layer on the skin.
  • Water – hydrates the slippery elm and guar gum.

Method

  1. Add: Add the slippery elm powder to the water and mix thoroughly.
  2. Hydrate: Allow it to hydrate and develop its natural mucilage.
  3. Mix: Mix in the vegetable glycerin.
  4. Thicken: Gradually add guar gum until the desired shaving-gel thickness is reached.
  5. Finish: Mix in the jojoba oil thoroughly.

Storage & Use

  • Store appropriately and use a suitable preservative if keeping the water-based gel for more than immediate use.

Comedogenic Considerations

  • The formula is predominantly water-based with only a small amount of jojoba oil. Individual skin response can still vary.
