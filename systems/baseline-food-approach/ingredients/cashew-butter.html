Cashew Butter

TypeTree-nut butter made from cashews (Anacardium occidentale)

Cashew butter is a smooth, naturally creamy nut paste with a mild flavour. Its relatively neutral taste makes it especially versatile in both savoury and sweet preparations.

Nutrition & composition

  • Provides unsaturated fat, plant protein and some fibre.
  • Contributes minerals including magnesium, copper and zinc.
  • Energy-dense, as expected for a concentrated nut product.

Culinary characteristics

  • Very smooth texture and mild flavour make it useful for creamy sauces and spreads.
  • Blends readily with water into a pale emulsion.
  • Works in sweet applications without an aggressively nutty flavour.

Uses & preparation

  • Spreads, porridges and desserts.
  • Creamy sauces, dips, soups and dressings.
  • Baking and blended fillings.

Selection, storage & considerations

  • Natural products may separate; stir before use.
  • Keep sealed and away from heat and light; follow label guidance after opening.
  • Tree-nut allergen.
